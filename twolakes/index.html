Good questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can send a text, you can use AI. We start wherever you are, and I leave you with notes written for you.

Which AI should I pay for?

It depends on what you do and what you already own. Part of the first visit is choosing one that fits, so you aren't paying for three.

Is it safe to connect AI to my email or business accounts?

It can be, when it's set up with care. I'll show you exactly what it can reach, keep you in control of anything that sends or spends, and skip anything you aren't comfortable with.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It will sometimes. That's why we build in checks: drafts you review, summaries that link to their source, and limits on what runs unattended.

Do you come to me?

Yes, anywhere around Lake Minnetonka and Waconia. Remote sessions work well too, wherever you are.
